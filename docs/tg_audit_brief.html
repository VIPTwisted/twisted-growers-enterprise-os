Standing requirements — set by the owner, non-negotiable
Requirement 01

Payroll is per-employee, at actual rates

Everyone is paid differently — the flat $22/hr entries are placeholders. Budgets roll up from people: each employee’s real rate × allocation × hours, with OT and 12% burden, to department and company totals. Per-role planned rates for open seats. Pay data stays on restricted executive surfaces only.

Requirement 02

Everything links to a COA; all testing tracked

A COA registry (lab, sample ID, dates, panel results, pass/fail, durable file link) keyed from every lot, batch, and shipment. A viewable COA schedule: submissions this week, at-lab, results due, QA queue, releases vs ship commitments. Hard gate: nothing goes Ready To Ship without a linked passing COA.
